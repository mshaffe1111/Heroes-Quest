# Heroes Quest: Army guide

Every number in this guide is read from the game rules.

## Tab: Armies

### How to read an army

- **Strength** is the number that wins duels. An army never has more than 9 before stack bonuses, and never more than 14 after them.
- **Moves** are move points, not squares. Open ground costs 2 a square, a road 1, woods 3, hills 4. A stack moves at the pace of its slowest army, and up to 2 unused points carry over to the next turn.
- **Cost** is paid once; **upkeep** is 50% of the price, rounded down, paid every turn while the army lives. Armies afloat cost 1 gold more.
- **Pips** are the hits an army can take before it falls. A pip lost in a battle stays lost (see Rules of battle).
- **Built in** is how many turns a city needs to finish the army.

### Ranks

An ordinary army earns ranks by winning duels. After 4 wins it becomes **Seasoned** and gains +1 pip. After 10 wins it becomes a **Veteran** and also gains +1 strength. Heroes do not earn ranks: they level up instead.

### Counters

In Heroes Quest, a few armies are good against particular enemies: they fight +1 stronger in the duel against them. That is small on purpose. The duel in the example below shows how terrain can swing it more than a counter can.

- **Light Cavalry** are strong against Elven Archers and Catapult.
- **Elven Archers** are strong against Giant Bat, Pegasi, Griffins and Dragon.
- **Pikemen** are strong against Light Cavalry, Heavy Cavalry and Wolf Riders.
- **Heavy Cavalry** are strong against Elven Archers and Catapult.
- **Giants** are strong against Pikemen.

Example, worked out exactly: Heavy Infantry attacking Light Infantry on open ground wins the duel 84% of the time. Heavy Cavalry attacking Pikemen wins 50% on open ground, but only 37% in woods, because Pikemen counter horse and the horse lose their open-ground bonus.

### What the classes are for

- **Foot** soldiers are the cheap backbone. Their price buys the most strength.
- **Mounted** armies are fast and shine on open ground and roads.
- **Beasts** are expensive and strong, and the biggest of them are slow to build.
- **Winged** armies fly over water and mountains, so they reach places nothing else can.
- **Siege** is for taking walled cities.
- **Special** armies cannot be built. They come from ruins, temples and quests.

### Foot

#### Scout
**Strength 1 · Moves 16 · Cost 2 gold · Upkeep 1 gold a turn · 2 pips · Built in 1 turn**
- Pays the open-ground move cost in woods and hills, and so does every army travelling with it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** Exploring and claiming ground cheaply. It slips through woods and hills, and it drags any stack it travels with through them at open-ground speed.
- **Watch out for:** It dies to almost anything. Never send it into a fight you can avoid.

#### Light Infantry
**Strength 2 · Moves 12 · Cost 2 gold · Upkeep 1 gold a turn · 2 pips · Built in 1 turn**
- On open ground its moves carry it 6 squares a turn, on a road 12.
- **Good for:** Cheap garrison filler and cheap bodies to soak up hits at the front of a stack.
- **Watch out for:** Very few pips. Anything heavier cuts through it.

#### Orc Mob
**Strength 2 · Moves 12 · Cost 3 gold · Upkeep 1 gold a turn · 2 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Pays the open-ground move cost in woods and hills, and so does every army travelling with it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 6 squares a turn, on a road 12.
- **Good for:** A cheap soldier that moves quickly through woods and hills, and carries a stack with it.
- **Watch out for:** Weak in a straight fight; use it for numbers, not for winning battles.

#### Heavy Infantry
**Strength 3 · Moves 8 · Cost 5 gold · Upkeep 2 gold a turn · 3 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- On open ground its moves carry it 4 squares a turn, on a road 8.
- **Good for:** A dependable soldier that holds a line and costs little for what it brings.
- **Watch out for:** No special bonus anywhere, so a good counter will beat it.

#### Elven Archers
**Strength 3 · Moves 12 · Cost 7 gold · Upkeep 3 gold a turn · 3 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Fights +1 stronger in woods (this army only).
- Pays the open-ground move cost in woods, and so does every army travelling with it.
- Strong against Giant Bat, Pegasi, Griffins and Dragon: it fights +1 stronger in those duels.
- Weak against Light Cavalry and Heavy Cavalry: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 6 squares a turn, on a road 12.
- **Good for:** Fighting in woods, where it is strong and travels quickly. It counters every flying army.
- **Watch out for:** Cavalry ride it down on open ground.

#### Dwarves
**Strength 3 · Moves 8 · Cost 7 gold · Upkeep 3 gold a turn · 3 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Fights +1 stronger in hills (this army only).
- Pays the open-ground move cost in hills, and so does every army travelling with it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 4 squares a turn, on a road 8.
- **Good for:** Fighting in hills, where it is strong and moves quickly. A sturdy defender of hill country.
- **Watch out for:** Ordinary value everywhere else; pick it for hill country, not for the plains.

#### Pikemen
**Strength 4 · Moves 8 · Cost 5 gold · Upkeep 2 gold a turn · 3 pips · Built in 3 turns**
- Fights +1 stronger on open ground and roads (this army only).
- Strong against Light Cavalry, Heavy Cavalry and Wolf Riders: it fights +1 stronger in those duels.
- Weak against Giants: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 4 squares a turn, on a road 8.
- **Good for:** The best value among the heavy foot. Strong on open ground and roads, and it counters every horse army.
- **Watch out for:** Giants hurl pikemen aside, and it is slow.

### Mounted

#### Light Cavalry
**Strength 2 · Moves 20 · Cost 4 gold · Upkeep 2 gold a turn · 2 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Fights +1 stronger on open ground and roads (this army only).
- Strong against Elven Archers and Catapult: it fights +1 stronger in those duels.
- Weak against Pikemen: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 10 squares a turn, on a road 20.
- **Good for:** Fast, and sharp on open ground and roads. It counters Elven Archers and Catapults.
- **Watch out for:** Pikemen counter it, and it gets no bonus in woods, hills or cities.

#### Heavy Cavalry
**Strength 4 · Moves 16 · Cost 8 gold · Upkeep 4 gold a turn · 3 pips · Built in 3 turns**
- Fights +2 stronger on open ground and roads (this army only).
- Strong against Elven Archers and Catapult: it fights +1 stronger in those duels.
- Weak against Pikemen: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** Hitting hard on open ground and roads while still moving fast. It counters Elven Archers and Catapults.
- **Watch out for:** Pikemen counter it, and in woods, hills or cities it loses its edge.

#### Wolf Riders
**Strength 4 · Moves 16 · Cost 8 gold · Upkeep 4 gold a turn · 3 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Lifts the whole stack by +1 in hills. Only the best single stack bonus counts.
- Weak against Pikemen: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** Fast, and it lifts the whole stack in hill country.
- **Watch out for:** Pikemen counter it, and the bonus only helps in the hills.

### Beasts

#### Giants
**Strength 5 · Moves 16 · Cost 13 gold · Upkeep 6 gold a turn · 4 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Pays the open-ground move cost in hills, and so does every army travelling with it.
- Strong against Pikemen: it fights +1 stronger in those duels.
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** Raw strength for the price, quick through hills, and it counters Pikemen.
- **Watch out for:** It is dear, so losing one hurts.

#### Minotaurs
**Strength 5 · Moves 12 · Cost 13 gold · Upkeep 6 gold a turn · 4 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Fights +1 stronger in cities, ruins, temples and camps (this army only).
- On open ground its moves carry it 6 squares a turn, on a road 12.
- **Good for:** Defending and assaulting built-up places: it is stronger in cities, ruins, temples and camps.
- **Watch out for:** No bonus in the open.

#### Giant Spiders
**Strength 5 · Moves 12 · Cost 16 gold · Upkeep 8 gold a turn · 4 pips · Built in 2 turns**
- Fights +2 stronger in cities, ruins, temples and camps (this army only).
- On open ground its moves carry it 6 squares a turn, on a road 12.
- **Good for:** Fighting in and around cities, ruins and temples, where its bonus is large.
- **Watch out for:** Dear, and no bonus away from built-up ground.

#### Unicorns
**Strength 7 · Moves 16 · Cost 19 gold · Upkeep 9 gold a turn · 4 pips · Built in 4 turns**
- Lifts the whole stack by +1 wherever it fights. Only the best single stack bonus counts.
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** A strong army that lifts the whole stack wherever it fights.
- **Watch out for:** Very dear, slow to build, and it cannot fly.

#### Elephants
**Strength 8 · Moves 16 · Cost 20 gold · Upkeep 10 gold a turn · 4 pips · Built in 4 turns**
- Every enemy army in the battle fights 1 weaker (never below one).
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** The strongest army you can build, and it weakens every enemy in the battle.
- **Watch out for:** Very dear and slow to build.

### Winged

#### Giant Bat
**Strength 1 · Moves 16 · Cost 5 gold · Upkeep 2 gold a turn · 2 pips · Built in 1 turn**

- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.

- **Watch out for:** Fragile, and Elven Archers shoot it down.

#### Pegasi

- Lifts the whole stack by +1 wherever it fights. Only the best single stack bonus counts.

- Weak against Elven Archers: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 10 squares a turn, on a road 20.
- **Good for:** It flies, and it lifts the whole stack wherever it fights.
- **Watch out for:** Dear, and Elven Archers counter it.

#### Griffins
**Strength 6 · Moves 20 · Cost 21 gold · Upkeep 10 gold a turn · 4 pips · Built in 3 turns**
- Fights +2 stronger in cities, ruins, temples and camps (this army only).
- Flies: ignores water and mountains and pays 1 move point a square.
- Weak against Elven Archers: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 10 squares a turn, on a road 20.
- **Good for:** A strong flyer that is even stronger in cities, ruins and temples.
- **Watch out for:** Dear, and Elven Archers counter it.

### Siege

#### Catapult
**Strength 2 · Moves 16 · Cost 15 gold · Upkeep 7 gold a turn · 2 pips · Built in 4 turns**
- Cancels the city and camp defence bonus of the defenders it attacks.
- Weak against Light Cavalry and Heavy Cavalry: they fight +1 stronger against it.
- On open ground its moves carry it 8 squares a turn, on a road 16.
- **Good for:** Taking walled cities: it cancels the defence bonus of cities and camps for the whole attacking side.
- **Watch out for:** Dear, weak in a duel and slow to build. Cavalry counter it, so guard it with something sturdy.

## Tab: Heroes

### Meeting a hero

Each turn there is a one in 6 chance that a hero offers to join you, if you can pay his price. A realm can have up to 5 heroes, or 6 while it holds a majority of the cities.

The price rises with the number of heroes you already have:

| Heroes you have | Price in gold |
|---|---|
| 0 | 100 |
| 1 | 300 |
| 2 | 450 |
| 3 | 600 |
| 4 | 750 |
| 5 | 900 |

### Levels

A hero reaches level 4 at most. Each level adds +1 strength and +2 moves, and the hero gains a pip at level 3.

| Level | Experience needed | Strength | Moves | Pips | Command bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 5 | 14 | 4 | +1 |
| 2 | 15 | 6 | 16 | 4 | +1 |
| 3 | 30 | 7 | 18 | 5 | +2 |
| 4 | 60 | 8 | 20 | 5 | +2 |

### Command

A hero lifts every army in his stack: the **command bonus**. It depends on his strength: 4 or more gives +1, 7 or more gives +2 and 9 or more gives +3. Command items add to it, and the best hero in the stack counts. The total stack bonus never passes 5.

### Experience

- Surviving a battle on the winning side: 1 experience.
- Taking a city: 2 experience.
- Exploring a ruin: 3 experience.
- Being blessed at a temple: 1 experience.
- Studying at a library (once per library): 2 experience.
- Finishing a quest: 10 experience.

### Perks

Each time a hero levels up he earns a perk. The game offers a shortlist of 3 and he keeps one. A hero can end with 3 perks. Each hero leans toward a style of play, and his shortlist leans the same way.

- **Rally:** In the first two duels of any battle the stack gets +1 stack bonus.
- **Charge:** In the first three duels of an attack the stack gets +1 stack bonus.
- **Fortifier:** In the first three duels when defending the stack gets +1 stack bonus.
- **Tactician:** Enemy counters do not work against this hero's stack, and his own stack's counters gain +1.
- **Banner-bearer:** The hero himself fights +1 stronger (not his stack).
- **Hardened:** The hero has +1 pip.
- **Pathfinder:** The hero gets +6 move points each turn, so a stack he leads travels faster.
- **Field Medic:** Wounded armies in his stack heal 1 pip every turn, even outside a city.

### Items

Heroes carry magical items found in ruins, from sages, as quest rewards and in strongholds. A hero who dies drops his items where he fell, and any hero can pick them up. If he dies in or over open water, they are lost. Heroes standing together can hand an item across.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Realm Standard | Adds +1 to the stack bonus. The realm banner. Plant it on open ground, a road, woods or hills, and you can send new armies straight to it. |
| Keen Blade | Adds +1 to the hero's strength (which can raise his command bonus too). |
| Dawnforged Blade | Adds +2 to the hero's strength (which can raise his command bonus too). |
| Sunsplitter | Adds +3 to the hero's strength (which can raise his command bonus too). |
| Captain's Pennant | Adds +1 to the stack bonus. |
| Banner of the Host | Adds +2 to the stack bonus. |
| Crown of Command | Adds +3 to the stack bonus. |
| Cloak of Wings | The whole stack flies. |
| Boots of Swiftness | The stack he ends the turn in gets 2 times the moves next turn, and a second speed hero in the same stack makes it 3 times. |
| Staff of Marches | The stack he ends the turn in gets 2 times the moves next turn, and a second speed hero in the same stack makes it 3 times. |
| Merchant's Purse | Brings in 10 gold a turn. |

### The Standard

Your first hero carries the Realm Standard. Plant it (not in a city, ruin, temple, on a mountain or in water) and you can set a city's new armies to march to it: up to 4 armies a turn reach it.

### A hero travelling with a stack

A hero moves at the pace of the armies he travels with. A hero alone, or any army that cannot fly, keeps the whole stack on the ground. A flying stack can take a hero with it.

## Tab: Terrain

### Move cost per square

| Ground | Move points |
|---|---|
| Road or bridge | 1 |
| Open ground | 2 |
| Woods | 3 |
| Hills | 4 |
| Swamp | 4 |
| City | 1 |
| Shallow water along a shore (boats) | 2 |
| Open sea (boats) | 1 |

Mountains and open water cannot be crossed on foot. Flying armies pay 1 point a square over anything.

Some armies pay the open-ground price in rough country, and carry the whole stack with them: Scout, Orc Mob and Elven Archers in woods and Scout, Orc Mob, Dwarves and Giants in hills.

### Terrain in battle

Some armies fight stronger on certain ground (their own strength only):

- **Open ground:** Light Cavalry +1, Pikemen +1 and Heavy Cavalry +2
- **Roads:** Light Cavalry +1, Pikemen +1 and Heavy Cavalry +2
- **Woods:** Elven Archers +1
- **Hills:** Dwarves +1
- **Cities, ruins, temples and camps:** Minotaurs +1, Giant Spiders +2 and Griffins +2

Stack bonuses that help every army in the stack:

- **Hills:** Wolf Riders +1
- **Anywhere:** Pegasi +1, Unicorns +1, Wizard +1, The Worm +1, Undead +1, Demon +1, Elemental +1, Devil +1, Archon +1 and Dragon +2

A road across open ground counts as both open ground and road. A road through woods or hills counts only as that wood or hill ground for battle bonuses.

### Defence bonuses

- A defending stack in **its own city** gets +1 if the city builds fewer than three army types, and +2 if it builds three or more.
- An **unsworn (neutral) city** gets +1 if it builds three or more types, and nothing otherwise. A razed city gives +0.
- **Ruins and temples** give +2; a **tower** gives +1.
- A stack that stays still for a full turn and is ordered to **defend** is encamped and gets +1 (not inside cities).

### By sea

Armies do not build ships: each army hires its own boat. A group takes to the boats at a coastal city, a port (the anchor squares) or a bridge, and that ends its move. It sails from the next turn. A group afloat sails open water, passes under bridges, and can come ashore only at a city, port or bridge, which also ends its move. Armies afloat cost 1 gold more in upkeep.

Boats can attack a coastal city from the water, and fight with their land strength. In a battle between boats at sea, every army fights at strength 4 with no bonuses, and counters do not apply. Flying armies ignore all of this. A lake is not coastal: nobody can put to sea from one.

## Tab: Cities and sites

### Cities

A city pays its income at the start of your turn, and each of your armies costs upkeep. A city can build up to 4 army types and works on one at a time. An army takes its build time in turns; gold for it is taken when it finishes, and if you are short the city waits (it also waits if the garrison is full). A city holds up to 32 armies; any other square holds 8.

To teach a city a new army type you pay its price times its build turns times 10. If all 4 slots are taken you choose one to replace.

New armies can be sent to another of your cities instead of staying put. They arrive after the build time plus 2 more turns.

### Taking a city

When you win a battle at a city you choose what to do with it:

- **Occupy:** it is yours as it stands.
- **Pillage:** it is yours, it loses its last army type, and you take 8 times its income in gold.
- **Sack:** it is yours but it loses every army type, and you take 12 times its income in gold.
- **Raze:** the city is destroyed and left as a ruin. Standing on the ruin with an army, you can rebuild it for 800 gold.

The Unsworn (neutral cities) normally keep a small garrison. An Unsworn city that has been attacked and holds out starts building defenders, one a turn, up to five. The Unsworn do not carry wounds from round to round.

### Ruins

Ruins are guarded by a special army. A hero searches a ruin alone or with a group: he faces the guardian on a single roll, and every other army in the group helps. If he wins, the ruin is explored (and cannot be used again). If he loses, he dies and the ruin stays unexplored.

His chance starts at 88%, rises by 3 points for each strength he has over the guardian, and by 2 points for each helper (up to seven), and never goes below 35% or above 98%.

A won ruin gives gold (150 to 600), an item, the guardian as a free ally, or a **sage** who offers up to three choices (gold, an item, or the location of a stronghold). If your stack has no room for the ally you are paid gold instead. **Strongholds** are ruins with strong guardians and the best items.

### Temples

Every army that visits a temple is blessed: +1 strength, once per temple. A blessed hero also gains experience, and may be offered a quest (50% chance).

### Libraries

A hero who studies at a library gains 2 experience, once per library.

### Quests

A realm can have one quest at a time, given to a particular hero, and it must be done as the attacker (being attacked never counts). The kinds are: take a named city; plunder cities for gold (300 to 900); slay a named enemy hero or one kind of special army; or destroy 10 to 25 armies of one rival. The reward is gold (200 to 500), an item, a special ally or the location of a stronghold, plus 10 experience for the hero.

## Tab: Rules of battle

### A duel

A battle is a string of **duels**. In each duel the front army of each side fights. A duel is a series of rounds. In each round both sides roll a die with 20 sides. A side whose roll is at or below the other side's strength loses the round, and loses a pip. If both sides lose or both win, they roll again. The duel ends when one army has no pips left. The loser is destroyed and the next army steps up.

The stronger army wins more rounds but never all of them: armies of equal strength and equal pips are even, and Heavy Infantry against Light Infantry wins 84% of the time. The odds advisor works these numbers out for you before you commit. An optional **Intense Combat** setting uses a die with 24 sides.

### Wounds

In Heroes Quest, pips an army loses stay lost: the army carries its wounds into the next duel and the next battle. Wounded armies heal only inside one of your own cities, 2 pips at the start of your turn. A hero with the Field Medic perk heals his stack's wounds 1 pip a turn anywhere.

### Strength in a duel

Each army's strength is: its own strength (with any bonus for the ground it stands on, never over 9), plus its side's **stack bonus**, plus a counter if it has one, less 1 if the other side has Elephants (never below one). It can never go over 14.

The stack bonus is the best hero's command bonus plus command items, plus the best single non-hero stack bonus, plus (for defenders) the city or camp bonus. It never goes over 5. Devils cancel the other side's non-hero bonuses, Archons cancel hero bonuses, and Catapults cancel the defenders' city and camp bonus. Bonuses are worked out again before every duel, so losing a bonus-giving army weakens the rest.

### Fight order

By default the weakest army in a stack fights first and the strongest last, with heroes always last. The army shown on top of a stack is the one that fights last. You can set your own fight order. Over mountains or open sea, a flying army is placed after the hero so he is never stranded.

### Calling it off, and falling back

After every duel in which both sides still have armies, the attacker may **call off the assault**: the survivors stay where they started, wounds and all. Defenders on open ground can give ground in the same way: they fall back to a neighbouring square, best of all their own city, and the attackers hold the field. Defenders in a city or on the water cannot.

### Heroes and battle

A hero gains 1 experience for being on the winning side. A hero who is destroyed drops his items.

### Limits

At most eight armies move or attack together, and at most eight stand on one square (32 in a city).
